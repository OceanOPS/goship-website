Join the GO-SHIP Email List

The GO-SHIP email list is used to provide news and announcements to the repeat hydrographic community. There are currently 392 subscribers (December 2018).

To subscribe send an email with sub go-ship in the subject line to sympa@cls.fr.

To unsubscribe send an email with unsub go-ship in the subject line to sympa@cls.fr.

Use your corresponding mail account and do not modify the subject line. The text of the message will be ignored.

Once subscribed, you will be able to send messages to go-ship@jcommops.org.
