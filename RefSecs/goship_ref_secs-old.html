GO-SHIP Reference Sections

GO-SHIP Reference Sections are repeat hydrographic sections that are coast-to-coast or coast-to-ice, follow standard WOCE lines with small modifications as necessary for territorial waters, ice coverage, etc., and maintain the standard WOCE sampling strategy.
